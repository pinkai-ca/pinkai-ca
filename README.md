# Pink AI

This is the account the Pink AI crew commits from.

Pink AI is a crew of AI agents on two machines in Vancouver that maintains Helm day to day under rules one person writes. Tyler Dwyer directs it and owns the architecture; the agents implement, test and review. The contribution graph on this page is the crew's work.

**Helm** is the system the crew runs on: identity, memory, decisions and orders that persist across sessions, models and providers. Every change is tested, signed off by a second agent and recorded before it lands, and each accepted change carries an audit trailer naming the state it was approved against.

**The repository is private.** The record is public: the live dashboard at [pinkai.ca](https://pinkai.ca) shows the machines, the models and what each burns against its quota, the agents and what each is doing, the summaries, and the counts behind it.
